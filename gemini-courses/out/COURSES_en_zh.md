# English live calls for Chinese speakers

48 scenario calls, A1 and A2. The character speaks English; the learner gets Chinese support and Chinese feedback.

## First days in Kraków (A1)

- **Hello, Neighbour** · 你好，新邻居 — with Marek: Say hello and give your name; Say where you are from; Ask him a question
- **Your First Coffee** · 你的第一杯咖啡 — with Ola: Order a drink; Order something to eat; Ask how much it is and pay
- **A New Colleague** · 新同事 — with Ania: Say what your job is; Say where you live; Ask her a question
- **How Are You?** · 你最近怎么样？ — with Kasia: Say how you are; Say what you are doing right now; Ask what she is doing

## Numbers and shopping (A1)

- **Tram Tickets** · 买电车票 — with Zbyszek: Ask for tickets: say how many and what kind; Ask the price; Pay and say thank you
- **At the Market** · 集市买菜 — with Basia: Buy a kilo of apples; Buy one more thing; Ask the total and pay
- **Swapping Numbers** · 交换联系方式 — with Kasia: Give your phone number; Spell your email address; Ask for her number
- **Fresh Bread** · 新鲜出炉的面包 — with Ola: Ask for bread rolls: say how many; Ask what else is fresh today; Pay

## Food and eating out (A1)

- **Lunch at a Milk Bar** · 在平民食堂吃午饭 — with Halina: Order a soup; Order a main dish; Order a drink and pay
- **Dinner at Tomek's** · 在托梅克家做客 — with Tomek: Say what you like and don't like; Say you are full, or ask for more; Compliment the food
- **Party Shopping** · 派对采购 — with Kasia: Say what you need to buy; Say what you already have or don't have; Agree on drinks
- **The Wrong Order** · 弄错的点单 — with Ola: Say what you ordered; Say what is wrong; Accept her offer or ask for something else

## Getting around (A1)

- **Which way to the station?** · 去火车站怎么走？ — with Marek: Ask the way to the station; Ask if it is far; Thank him
- **A ticket to Warsaw** · 买一张去华沙的火车票 — with Grażyna: Ask for a ticket to Warsaw: single or return; Choose a departure time; Ask which platform it leaves from
- **Taxi to the hotel** · 乘出租车去酒店 — with Zbyszek: Say where you are going; Ask how long the ride takes; Ask for the price and pay
- **Where are you?** · 你在哪儿呢？ — with Kasia: Say where you are and what you can see; Follow her directions; Say when you will get there

## Home (A1)

- **Viewing a flat** · 看房 — with Basia: Ask how many rooms there are; Ask about the rent and bills; Say what you like or don't like
- **Show me your room** · 看看你的新房间 — with Kasia: Describe three things in your room; Say what is missing; Ask about her room
- **A morning run** · 早起晨跑 — with Tomek: Say what time you get up; Say what you do in the morning; Agree on a time to run
- **No hot water** · 没有热水了 — with Marek: Explain the problem; Ask what to do or who to call; Thank him

## Time and plans (A1)

- **When are you free?** · 你什么时候有空？ — with Kasia: Suggest a day; Agree on a time; Decide where to meet
- **Weekend in the mountains?** · 周末去山里徒步吗？ — with Tomek: Say what you usually do at weekends; Say if you can come; Ask what to bring
- **Booking a doctor's appointment** · 预约看医生 — with Krysia: Say you want an appointment; Agree on a day and time; Give your name
- **A birthday invitation** · 生日聚会邀请 — with Ania: Accept the invitation; Ask when and where it is; Ask what to bring

## Health (A2)

- **At the Doctor's** · 看医生 — with Dr Nowak: Describe your symptoms clearly.; Say how long you've felt unwell.; Ask a question about your treatment or medication.
- **At the Pharmacy** · 在药店买药 — with Ela: Explain what cold symptoms you have.; Ask for medicine that doesn't need a prescription.; Ask how and when to take it.
- **Calling in Sick** · 向领导请病假 — with Ania: Say you're unwell and can't come in today.; Briefly describe what's wrong.; Agree on what to do about today's meeting.
- **At the Gym** · 在健身房锻炼 — with Tomek: Say what sports you play and how often you exercise.; Say when an exercise feels too hard or painful.; Agree on a suitable workout plan together.

## What happened? (A2)

- **How Was Your Weekend?** · 周末过得怎么样？ — with Kasia: Mention two things you did over the weekend.; Say where you went.; Ask Kasia how her weekend went.
- **A Trip to Gdańsk** · 格但斯克海滨之旅 — with Marek: Explain how you travelled there and how long it took.; Describe what sights you saw.; Share what you enjoyed the most.
- **You Won't Believe It!** · 你绝对想不到！ — with Tomek: Tell a short story about an unexpected thing that happened to you.; React naturally to his story.; Use sequence words: first, then, and finally.
- **A Stolen Wallet** · 钱包失窃报案 — with Officer Lis: Explain what happened and approximately when it occurred.; Describe the wallet and list what was inside it.; Provide your phone number and contact details.

## Shops and services (A2)

- **Returning a Jacket** · 退换衣服 — with Monika: State that you want to return or exchange the jacket.; Explain why it doesn't fit.; Ask to try a bigger size or another colour.
- **Sending a Parcel** · 在邮局寄国际包裹 — with Wiesia: State the destination country of the parcel.; Choose between standard or express delivery.; Answer questions about the parcel's contents.
- **Opening a Bank Account** · 在银行开户 — with Tadeusz: Explain that you would like to open a bank account.; Provide your passport and address information.; Ask about debit card fees and monthly account charges.
- **At the Hairdresser's** · 在理发店理发 — with Jola: Explain what you want done with your hair.; Specify how short you want the sides and top.; Make friendly small talk about your week.

## Work and study (A2)

- **A Job Interview** · 求职面试 — with Dorota: Introduce yourself and your experience; Explain why you want the job; Ask one question about the role
- **Signing Up for a Language Course** · 报名语言班 — with Paweł: State your current level and learning goals; Choose your preferred days and times; Ask about the price and course materials
- **Asking for Help** · 向同事请教求助 — with Ania: Ask politely for assistance; Explain what the problem is; Agree on a convenient time to go over it
- **Rescheduling a Meeting** · 改约会议时间 — with Ania: Explain why you cannot make the meeting tomorrow; Suggest another day and time; Confirm the new appointment

## Travel (A2)

- **Checking In at a Guesthouse** · 入住波兰民宿 — with Adam: Check in using your reservation name; Ask about breakfast times and the Wi-Fi password; Report a problem with your room
- **Planning a Mountain Trip** · 筹划高山徒步之旅 — with Tomek: Agree on how to travel there; Choose where to spend the night; Split the tasks and decide who gets what
- **What's the Weather Like?** · 出门前聊天气 — with Marek: Tell him where you are going and when; Ask about the weather forecast; Mention what gear or clothes you will take
- **A Problem with the Food** · 就餐出现问题 — with Kuba: Explain politely what is wrong with your food; Ask for something else instead; Ask for the bill

## Opinions and culture (A2)

- **Movie Night** · 电影之夜 — with Kasia: Say what types of movies you like and why; Politely disagree with her choice; Agree on one film to watch
- **Christmas Eve Dinner** · 平安夜家宴 — with Marek: Thank him warmly and accept the invitation; Ask about traditional Polish Christmas Eve food; Explain how you celebrate holidays back home
- **Kraków or Warsaw?** · 克拉科夫还是华沙？ — with Ania: Compare the two cities on two different points; Give your opinion with clear reasons; Find something both of you can agree on
- **Plans and Dreams** · 未来的梦想与规划 — with Kasia: Share what you plan to do next year; Describe what you would do with more time or money; Ask her about her own future dreams
