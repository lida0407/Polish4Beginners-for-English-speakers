What's inside

Spaced repetition

Leitner scheduling: words you know return after 1, 3, 7, 16 then 35 days. Words you miss come back the same day.

Real-life conversations

33 scenarios — trains, hotels, shops, restaurants, the doctor, reporting a lost passport — with line-by-line audio.

Hands-free listening

Each word twice in Polish, once in English, then a pause. Put the phone down and keep going.

Declension tables

All seven cases, singular and plural, on 100 common nouns — the part of Polish that actually trips people up.

Tap any word

Tap a Polish word in a conversation to translate it, hear it, and save it to a list or your favourites.

Your own material

Import word lists, dictionaries and your own conversations as CSV or JSON. Blank sides translate themselves.

Grammar and alphabet

23 short lessons with self-checks, plus all 39 letters and digraphs with pronunciation.

Polish news

Headlines from Polish outlets with in-app translation — reading practice that stays current.

Offline by design

Everything except the news feed works with no connection. Vocabulary, audio, grammar and translation all run on the device. Translation uses Google's ML Kit, which downloads a language model once and then works offline.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. The app collects nothing about you. Your progress never leaves your phone. Read the privacy policy →

Who it's for

  • English speakers living in or moving to Poland
  • Travellers who want phrases that work in a real shop or ticket office
  • Anyone who prefers studying without a subscription or a login

Bring your own dictionary

The app ships no dictionary. Upload your own in CSV, TSV or JSON and the app builds every translation once, on your device, so lookups stay instant. Format guide →
