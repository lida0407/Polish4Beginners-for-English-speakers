Privacy Policy

Polish4Beginners — Android app

Last updated: 10 August 2026

In short: Polish4Beginners does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you study stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect or transmit personal information. Specifically:

Account or sign-inNot required. The app has no login.
Name, email, phoneNever requested.
AnalyticsNo analytics SDK is present.
Crash reportingNo crash reporting SDK is present.
AdvertisingNo ads and no advertising identifiers.
LocationNot accessed. No location permission is requested.
ContactsNot accessed.
Camera / microphoneNot accessed.
Photos and mediaNot accessed. No storage permission is requested.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your learning data locally, in private app storage that other apps cannot read:

  • Study progress and review scheduling for each card
  • Favourites and your custom word lists
  • Dictionaries and conversation files you choose to import
  • Settings such as theme, interface language, speech speed and voice
  • Cached translations generated on your device

This information is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app removes it.

3. Permissions

INTERNETThe only permission the app requests. Used to download vocabulary updates, fetch Polish news headlines when you open the News tab, and let Google ML Kit download its offline translation model.

4. Network connections

The app makes read-only requests. It never uploads your data.

  • GitHub — checks for and downloads vocabulary database updates.
  • Polish news publishers — public RSS feeds, fetched only when you open the News tab. Opening a full article hands off to your browser.
  • Google ML Kit — downloads a Polish–English translation model the first time translation is used.

As with any internet request, the servers contacted can see your device's IP address. No identifying information about you is sent by the app.

5. Translation

Translation uses Google's ML Kit. Once the language model has been downloaded, translation runs entirely on your device — the text you translate is not sent to a server. ML Kit is delivered through Google Play Services; Google's own handling of model delivery is covered by Google's ML Kit terms.

6. Text-to-speech

Spoken audio is produced by the text-to-speech engine installed on your device (for example Google's, or another engine you have chosen). Whether a given voice works offline or connects to a network is determined by that engine, not by this app. Voices requiring a connection are labelled "online" in the app's voice picker.

7. Files you import

You can import word lists, dictionaries and conversation files. You pick each file explicitly through Android's system file picker; the app has no general access to your storage. Imported content stays on your device and is never uploaded.

8. Backup

If you have enabled Android's backup, your study progress and settings may be included in your own Google account backup. This is standard Android functionality; we do not operate any backup service. Regenerable caches are excluded from backup.

9. Children

The app is a general-audience language-learning tool. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
